## Description

Add a coin tester for Hedera.

Fourteen coin testers exist and Hedera is not one of them. Every family already on the generic adapter
but one has a tester, and it is the cheapest way to get deterministic coverage of the migrated
surface.

**Build it against the generic bridge from the start.** A tester that only constructs the legacy
bridge proves nothing about this migration — that mistake was made on another family and had to be
corrected. The Cosmos tester is a good model: it builds both bridges and feeds the generic one its own
signer adapter.

Cover the flows this epic changes: two syncs in a row, send, an HTS transfer, an ERC-20 transfer,
association, and the staking operations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A coin tester exists for Hedera and constructs the generic account bridge
- [ ] Scenarios cover two consecutive syncs, send, both token standards, association and staking
- [ ] The tester runs green locally and is wired into the repository's tester setup

Split the countervalues Redux slice by owner. The rates now have their own slice in `@domain/entity-market-countervalues` (`countervaluesRatesSlice`, with `setCountervaluesState` and `wipeCountervalues`), stored with a shallow freeze so a load never pays for a deep freeze of every rate. `@features/platform-market-countervalues` keeps the loop's state in `countervaluesSyncSlice` (`load`, `error`, `isPolling`; `load` is one of `idle`, `requested`, `loading`, `loadingThenRequested`, replacing the former `pending` and `triggerLoad` flags) and mounts both under the same `countervalues` store key through `countervaluesReducer`, typed `CountervaluesStoreState`, which replaces `CountervaluesState`. The store reads `countervalues.rates` and `countervalues.sync` instead of `countervalues.countervalues.state` and `countervalues.polling`; persistence and the saved data are unchanged. Each slice now declares its own actions, so the `COUNTERVALUES_*` type strings are gone: `setCountervaluesState` and `wipeCountervalues` keep their names, typed `countervaluesRates/set` and `countervaluesRates/wipe`, and the loop's setters `setCountervaluesPollingTriggerLoad`, `setCountervaluesStatePending`, `setCountervaluesStateError` and `setCountervaluesPollingIsPolling` become the events `countervaluesLoadRequested`, `countervaluesLoadStarted`, `countervaluesLoadSucceeded`, `countervaluesLoadFailed` and `countervaluesPollingSet`, typed `countervaluesSync/...`. A load start and a load failure now dispatch one action each instead of two. The sync selectors are the slice's own `selectors`, and `countervaluesSyncTriggerLoadSelector` is gone. `useCountervaluesPolling` now returns only the `poll`, `start`, `stop` and `wipe` commands and no longer re-renders its callers on every load; `pending` and `error` come from the new `useCountervaluesLoadStatus`, which live-common's `useSyncSources` now reads.

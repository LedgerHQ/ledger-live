Account Detail shows the shared empty state instead of the Assets and Transactions sections when the account has no activity (no operation or pending operation, on the account or its tokens), on LWD and LWM. The hero stays above it. On LWM, an account without funds now offers Transfer · Swap · Buy (Swap opens with the account as destination), and with Swap a lone active Buy or Sell is shown as its own tile instead of More.
